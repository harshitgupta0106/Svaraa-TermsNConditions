The Story Behind Svaraa

Personal Story: Brother with Sisters' Experiences

Growing up with three sisters, I witnessed firsthand the challenges and stigma surrounding menstrual health. I saw how they struggled to find reliable, non-judgmental information about their bodies and health concerns. The lack of accessible, stigma-free resources for teen girls was evident, and I realized that technology could bridge this gap.

As a brother, I wanted to create something that would empower my sisters and millions of other girls to take control of their health journey with confidence and knowledge. This personal connection to the problem drove me to build Svaraa.

Why Svaraa was Built

Svaraa was born from the urgent need to address the silence and stigma around women's health, particularly among teenagers. Many girls face:

  • Lack of accessible health information
  • Fear and embarrassment about discussing health concerns
  • Difficulty recognizing early signs of common conditions like PCOS and UTIs
  • Limited access to reliable, age-appropriate health resources

Svaraa aims to break these barriers by providing a discreet, interactive, and stigma-free health mentor that empowers girls with knowledge, confidence, and control over their health journey.

Features: Svaraa's Talk, Life, Plan

🗣️ Svaraa's Talk

Interactive chat powered by Apple's Foundation Models, providing personalized health guidance and answering questions in a safe, judgment-free environment.

📱 Life

Comprehensive health tracking and monitoring tools to help users recognize patterns and early signs of potential health concerns.

📋 Plan

Personalized health plans and recommendations tailored to individual needs, helping users take proactive steps toward better health.

Technical Innovation

Svaraa leverages cutting-edge Apple technologies to deliver a seamless, private, and powerful user experience:

  • App Intents: Seamless integration with iOS system features and third-party apps
  • SwiftUI: Modern, responsive UI built with Apple's declarative framework
  • Foundation Models: AI-powered chat experience using Apple's on-device machine learning
  • Privacy-First Design: All data processing happens locally on the user's device

Mission: Breaking Stigma Around Menstrual Health

Our mission is to create a world where every girl feels empowered to take control of her health journey. We believe that knowledge is power, and by providing accessible, accurate, and stigma-free health information, we can help girls:

  • Recognize early signs of health concerns
  • Build confidence in discussing health topics
  • Make informed decisions about their well-being
  • Access support and resources when needed

Achievement: Apple Swift Student Challenge Winner

Svaraa was recognized as a winning app in the Apple Swift Student Challenge, highlighting its innovation, technical excellence, and potential to make a positive impact on users' lives.

This recognition validates our mission and motivates us to continue developing solutions that empower girls with knowledge and confidence.
