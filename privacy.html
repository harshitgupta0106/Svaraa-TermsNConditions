Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At Svaraa, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

We believe that your health information is personal and private, and we have designed Svaraa with privacy-first principles to ensure your data remains secure and confidential.

2. Information Collected

Svaraa is designed with privacy in mind. We collect no personal data, no tracking, and no sharing of your information with third parties.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Health data that could identify you personally

Any health-related information you input into the app remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no data to use, store, or process. The app functions entirely on your device, ensuring that your health conversations and data remain private and secure.

Any AI-powered features use Apple's Foundation Models locally on your device, meaning your conversations never leave your phone.

4. Data Security

Svaraa is built with privacy-first principles. We have implemented the following security measures:

  • All data processing happens locally on your device
  • No data transmission to external servers
  • Encryption of local data storage
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • Compliance with Apple's privacy guidelines

5. User Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's no data to access, modify, or delete. However, you have complete control over your local data:

  • You can delete the app and all associated data at any time
  • You can clear app data through your device settings
  • You can stop using the app without any data retention concerns

6. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be notified via app release notes.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
